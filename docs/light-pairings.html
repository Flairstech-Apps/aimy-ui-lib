Which colour sits on which

Every ground the library draws in the light theme, with the inks and tints that land on it, from the live tokens. Each ink here passes AA on each ground it is shown on.

  • Warm on white, never on the grey ground. A 10% amber or red tint over a cool grey turns beige and dusty pink (its colour drops by a third); over white it stays amber and red. Status grounds belong inside cards.
  • Violet and blue light on a neutral ground. A deep violet at low strength over a slate ground goes lilac-grey. The light theme's ground is now near neutral (#f4f5f8), and glows use lighter, saturated hues.
  • Ink on tint: its own family, darker. Green text on the green tint, never grey text on a tint, so the tint reads as meaning, not as a stain.
  • One brand blue, one AI violet. Blue is the action; violet is what AiMY does. Never both on the same ground at the same weight.
  • Lift with edge and shadow, not with grey. White cards on the light ground need their edge; a second grey step under them reads dirty.
