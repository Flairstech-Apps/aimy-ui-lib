Table variants

The DataTable's table form today: in dark a zebra of the card and raised grounds; in light no zebra at all (both grounds are white), no rule under the head, no row hover, and every cell in secondary ink. Three directions, each in both themes. Hover a row; the third row is selected.

A

Ruled

One card with its edge. A sunken head band with a rule under it, a hairline between rows, a wash on hover. The first column in primary ink names the row. Reads as a record table: dense, scannable, the same in both themes.

B

Banded

No card and no rules: the head sits on the page and every other row is a soft rounded band (a white band in light). Lightest: suits a table inside a card or a chat reply, where a second card edge would double up.

C

Rows as cards

Today's rows form taken to the table: every row its own card with an edge, a gap between. Roomiest: suits a short list of things you act on one at a time (runs, contacts to call), less so a 50-row record.
