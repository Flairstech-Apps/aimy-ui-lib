Presentations: two storytelling templates

Horizon and Two lanes, both going into the library as separate templates, now with motion graphics. Each tells the same story, the library's own from 27 October to now. Every deck works: click it, then use the arrow keys or Space, or press Present to see it full screen.

  • The logo moves the way it does in the extension. On the cover the mark sits large in its halo. Leaving the cover, it winds up, flies to the top left in an arc with one full turn, lands with a small spring, and the word AiMY wipes out from behind it. It flies back for the close, so the deck opens and ends on the mark.
  • AiMY thinks between moments. Each time you move on, the header mark comes apart into dots, turns once, and gathers back: the extension's thinking mark, cut to about a second.
  • The timeline moves with you. In Horizon the orb hops along the horizon to the next mark, its light slides across the ground, a light runs along the horizon ahead of it and the mark rings when it arrives. In Two lanes the same light runs along the axis, the line fills behind you, the arrow draws from what we learned to what we did, and that card lands with a spring.
  • Underneath: the extension's ambient field. Two blooms drift on 29 and 37 second cycles and dust rises through the slide. In Horizon the dust rises out of the horizon.
  • Numbers count up to their value when their slide arrives, then hold. Titles arrive word by word.
  • One call for you: motion timing. The UI tokens stop at 500ms. The flight uses the extension's own 720ms, the orb's hop 760ms and the count 1.1s. I'd make these a small set of deck motion values in the library rather than stretch the UI ones.
